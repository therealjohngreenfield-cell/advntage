Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there a $1,000 minimum ad spend?

Below $1,000/month in ad spend, it's very difficult to gather enough data to optimize campaigns effectively. We want every client to see real results, and that requires a meaningful budget to work with. The ad spend goes directly to Google and Facebook — none of it goes to us.

What does "exclusive territory" mean exactly?

We will never take on a client in the same business category within a 15-mile radius of your location. This means we're 100% in your corner — never helping a direct competitor.

Are there long-term contracts?

We don't lock you into long-term contracts. We work on a month-to-month basis after an initial 90-day commitment. We keep clients through performance, not contracts.

What does the client dashboard show?

Your client dashboard gives you real-time visibility into your campaigns — leads generated, cost per lead, ad spend, impressions, clicks, and conversion rates. You can check in any time without needing to log into Google or Meta directly.

How quickly can campaigns go live?

After onboarding, most campaigns are live within 7–10 business days. This includes campaign buildout, landing page creation, tracking setup, and ad creative.

What types of home service businesses do you work with?

We work with roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, cleaning, pest control, painting, windows & doors, and more. If you're generating leads for a service performed at or around the home, we can help.
