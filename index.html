Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there a $1,000 minimum ad spend? +
Below $1,000/month in ad spend, it's very difficult to gather enough data to optimize campaigns effectively. We want every client to see real results, and that requires a meaningful budget to work with. The ad spend goes directly to Google and Facebook — none of it goes to us.
What does "exclusive territory" mean exactly? +
We will never take on a client in the same business category within a 15-mile radius of your location. For example, if we're running ads for a roofing company in your area, we won't work with any other roofer in that zone. This means we're 100% in your corner — never helping a direct competitor.
Are there long-term contracts? +
We don't lock you into long-term contracts. We work on a month-to-month basis after an initial 90-day commitment (campaigns need time to optimize and deliver results). We keep clients through performance, not contracts.
What does the client dashboard show? +
Your client dashboard gives you real-time visibility into your campaigns — leads generated, cost per lead, ad spend, impressions, clicks, and conversion rates. You can check in any time and see exactly how your budget is performing without needing to log into Google or Meta directly.
How quickly can campaigns go live? +
After onboarding, most campaigns are live within 7–10 business days. This includes campaign buildout, landing page creation, tracking setup, and ad creative. We don't rush the setup — doing it right the first time saves time and money down the road.
What types of home service businesses do you work with? +
We work with a wide range of home service businesses including roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, cleaning, pest control, painting, windows & doors, and more. If you're generating leads for a service performed at or around the home, we can help.
