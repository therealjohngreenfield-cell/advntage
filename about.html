About Advntage

The story behind the name.

We built Advntage to give home service businesses the same quality paid media management that enterprise brands get — without the bloat, without the BS.

Advntage was built for home service businesses that are serious about growth. We're a boutique paid media agency specializing in Google and Facebook advertising — two platforms that, when managed correctly, can transform a local business.

The name says it all: Ad is right there at the front. It's what we do, it's in our DNA, and it's the unfair advantage we give every client we work with over their local competition.

Our core belief is simple: you shouldn't be competing against your own agency's other clients. That's why we operate on an exclusive territory model — one business sector, per 15-mile radius. When we're running ads for a roofer in your market, we're only running ads for you.

We handle everything end-to-end: campaign strategy, landing page development, Google Tag Manager setup, conversion tracking, Facebook ad creative, retargeting sequences, weekly reporting, and a live client dashboard so you always know exactly what's happening.

No long-term contracts. No vanity metrics. No fluff. Just qualified leads and transparent reporting.
