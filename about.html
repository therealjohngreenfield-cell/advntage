About Us

We don't sell ads.
We sell results.

A boutique paid media agency built to drive real leads for home service businesses in upstate New York.

Our Story

Advntage was born from frustration. We watched home service businesses throw money at paid ads that didn't work — broad keywords, poor targeting, zero tracking, and no accountability. Meanwhile, agencies were more interested in volume than results.

So we built something different. A small, specialized team focused exclusively on one thing: getting you qualified leads at a cost that makes sense. No 50-client accounts. No excuses. Just strategy, execution, and weekly proof that it's working.
