Reliable Movers LLC — March 2026 Google Ads Summary

reliablemoversllc.com had a strong month — 2,205 impressions, 194 clicks, $1,037.17 spend. The campaign generated 59 conversions at $17.58 per conversion, broken down as 28 lead form submits, 19 quote requests, and 12 phone calls. Running on Maximize Conversions with a Target CPA, the .com campaign is optimizing effectively toward lead actions.

reliablemoversllc.net generated 2,502 impressions, 234 clicks, $1,428.36 spend with 16 tracked conversions at $89.27 each. The .net campaign runs on Maximize Clicks — optimizing for traffic volume rather than lead actions, which explains the higher cost per conversion. Phone calls (10 from ads + 2 smart campaign) are the primary tracked signal on .net, with limited form tracking on the .net domain.

Combined March total: $2,465.53 spend · 75 conversions · $32.87 blended cost per conversion. The .com campaign delivers significantly more efficient lead generation and received the larger budget allocation effective April 8 — $40/day vs $20/day for .net, out of the $1,800/month total.

Top converting keywords on .com: "moving company near me" (14 conversions) and "movers syracuse ny" (12 conversions). Key optimizations in March included match type upgrades on 40+ keywords, removal of 50 underperforming keywords, an RSA headline update, and negative keyword additions to filter out budget-seeking searchers effective April 8.
